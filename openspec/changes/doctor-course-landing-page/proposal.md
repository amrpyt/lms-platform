## Why

Dr. Zeyad Mohanna (دكتور زياده مهنا) requires a professional online presence to host and sell educational courses. This platform will serve as the central hub for his students to access content, manage enrollments, and view course materials.

## What Changes

We will build a comprehensive Course Platform (LMS) for Dr. Zeyad Mohanna. This includes:
- A professional landing page introducing the doctor and his teaching style.
- A course catalog showcasing available subjects.
- Student authentication (Login/Sign-up).
- A course player/viewer for enrolled students to access video content and materials.
- An administration interface for the doctor to manage courses and students.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `landing-page`: High-conversion entry page with Dr. Zeyad's profile and featured courses.
- `student-authentication`: Secure login and registration system for students.
- `course-catalog`: Browsable list of all available courses with filtering and search.
- `course-player`: Protected video player and document viewer for course content.
- `student-dashboard`: Personal area for students to see their enrolled courses and progress.
- `admin-dashboard`: Management interface for uploading courses, managing students, and tracking sales.

## Impact

- **Frontend**: New UI components for the landing page, course cards, and player.
- **Backend**: New API endpoints for auth, course retrieval, and progress tracking.
- **Database**: New schemas for Users (Students/Admin), Courses, Lessons, and Enrollments.
- **Assets**: Integration of Dr. Zeyad's professional photos and course thumbnails.
