## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Hero Section
The system SHALL display a hero section at the top of the landing page.
- It MUST include a high-quality image of Dr. Zeyad Mohanna.
- It MUST include a compelling headline (e.g., "Master Your Future with Dr. Zeyad Mohanna").
- It MUST include a Call to Action (CTA) button labeled "Browse Courses".

#### Scenario: User arrives at the site
- **WHEN** the user visits the root URL
- **THEN** they see Dr. Zeyad's photo and the "Browse Courses" button clearly visible in the hero area.

### Requirement: Course Catalog Grid
The system SHALL display a grid of featured courses.
- Each course card MUST show a title, short description, price, and thumbnail.
- Clicking a course card SHALL lead to a (mock) course detail page or enrollment modal.

#### Scenario: Browsing courses
- **WHEN** the user scrolls to the "Featured Courses" section
- **THEN** they see a responsive grid of cards representing different courses.

### Requirement: About the Doctor Section
The system SHALL include a section dedicated to Dr. Zeyad's biography and credentials.
- It MUST include a professional bio text.
- It MUST highlight key achievements (e.g., "10+ Years experience").

#### Scenario: Learning about the instructor
- **WHEN** the user scrolls to the "Meet Dr. Zeyad" section
- **THEN** they read his background and see his expertise highlighted.

### Requirement: Responsive Design
The landing page SHALL be fully responsive.
- The layout MUST adapt for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
- Navigation menus MUST become a "hamburger" menu on mobile devices.

#### Scenario: Viewing on mobile
- **WHEN** the user accesses the site from a smartphone
- **THEN** the content is stacked vertically and easily readable without horizontal scrolling.
